## Execution semantics

The I4 engine makes timing and cost assumptions explicit.

### No-lookahead timing

A signal is assumed to be generated at the close of a completed bar. It can execute only at the first available bar whose start is at or after the signal timestamp. The labelled fixture therefore enters `I4-S1` on the M1 bar starting at 12:01 after the preceding bar closes at 12:01.

No same-bar close-to-open hindsight is used.

### Transaction costs

The config contains explicit:

- spread in basis points;
- slippage in basis points;
- commission per order.

Half spread plus slippage is charged on each entry/exit side. Commission is charged on entry and exit. Tests compare the normal configuration with a zero-cost configuration and assert that explicit costs reduce reported performance.

### OHLC ambiguity

When a single OHLC bar touches both stop and target, the default policy is `STOP_FIRST`. This is intentionally conservative. The alternative `TARGET_FIRST` exists only as an explicit configuration choice.

### I3 risk-gate continuity

Signals whose `risk_status` is not `APPROVED` are not backtested as executable trades. The versioned fixture includes one `VETOED` signal and tests prove it is skipped.

## Out-of-sample discipline

### Chronological split

`chronological_split` creates ordered train/test intervals. Tests assert:

- train end <= test start;
- final train index < first test index.

No shuffle is used.

### Walk-forward

`walk_forward_windows` creates rolling train/test windows. The I4 protocol pins:

- train bars: 6;
- test bars: 3;
- step bars: 3.

Tests assert that every train interval finishes before its corresponding test interval starts.

The fixture validates split mechanics. It does not establish out-of-sample alpha because the fixture is synthetic and tiny.

## Explicit non-claims

I4 does **not** claim:

- profitability on real historical market data;
- statistically significant expectancy;
- real IG spreads or slippage distributions;
- out-of-sample alpha;
- robust Sharpe or Sortino values;
- independent parity with NautilusTrader or LEAN;
- calibrated ML probabilities;
- live-money readiness.

Sharpe/Sortino are intentionally omitted from the tiny synthetic fixture because producing them here would imply statistical precision the data cannot support.
